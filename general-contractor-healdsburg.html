    What shapes a Healdsburg project

    AreaWhat we plan around
    Plaza & downtown districtHistoric and design review on exterior changes. Late-1800s and early-1900s housing stock with the wiring, plumbing and foundation conditions that implies.
    Dry Creek & Alexander ValleyAgricultural parcels, vineyard frontage, well and septic, long private drives that must accommodate fire apparatus and material delivery.
    Fitch Mountain & hillside parcelsSlope stability, engineered or drilled-pier foundations, drainage, and WUI Chapter 7A fire-hardened assemblies.
    Russian River corridorMapped floodplain in places, bringing elevation and flood-resistant construction requirements.
    Geyserville & north countyUnincorporated — Permit Sonoma jurisdiction, with fire-access review on rural parcels.

    On vineyard and hillside parcels, walk the land before you buy. Access width, well yield, septic feasibility, slope and fire-apparatus turnaround decide far more of your budget than the finish schedule does. Site work on a steep Healdsburg parcel can exceed $300,000 — and that is knowable in an afternoon.

    Fire hardening in the Healdsburg hills

    Much of the area around Healdsburg is mapped Wildland-Urban Interface, and the 2019 Kincade Fire made the requirements concrete for a lot of local owners. Chapter 7A brings Class A roof assemblies, ember-resistant vents, ignition-resistant siding and soffits, enclosed eaves, tempered glazing and decking restrictions within ten feet of the structure. Budget $25,000–$70,000 on new construction.

    This is increasingly an insurance question as much as a code question. Carriers in this part of the county price and decline coverage on exactly these features, so a compliant build affects whether the finished home can be insured at all.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who builds custom homes in Healdsburg?

    Howard Construction Inc. is a family-owned Class B general contractor, CSLB #836369, building custom homes across Healdsburg, Dry Creek Valley and Alexander Valley since 2000. Verify any contractor you consider at cslb.ca.gov for active licence status, classification and insurance before signing anything.

    What does it cost to build a home in Healdsburg?

    Custom home construction runs $400 to $850 per square foot in 2026, excluding land. On vineyard and hillside parcels, site work adds $140,000 to $600,000 depending on slope, access, well and septic, and WUI fire-hardening adds $25,000 to $70,000. Healdsburg land prices sit at the upper end of Sonoma County.

    Do I need design review in downtown Healdsburg?

    Exterior alterations to properties in Healdsburg's historic and plaza districts generally require design review, including window replacement, siding, roofing and porch changes. This adds time to permitting and often requires period-appropriate materials. Interior work typically does not trigger review.

    Can I build an ADU on a Healdsburg vineyard property?

    In most cases yes. California law requires ministerial approval of compliant ADU applications with no public hearing and a 60-day decision deadline. On rural Healdsburg parcels the practical constraints are septic capacity, well yield and fire-apparatus access rather than zoning. Detached ADUs cost $180,000 to $420,000.
