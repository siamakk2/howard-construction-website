Small Town, Exacting Standards

Yountville may be barely a square mile, but it holds some of the most carefully maintained properties in Napa Valley — and homeowners here notice details. Reveal lines, cabinet alignment, stone book-matching, the way a pergola shadow falls across a patio at five in the afternoon. That's the level we build to, because in Yountville anything less looks out of place next to what's down the street.

We also understand what it means to run a jobsite in a town built on hospitality. Quiet hours matter. Clean streets matter. Parking that doesn't clog Washington Street matters. Our crews stage tightly, protect landscaping, and leave the site presentable every single evening — habits Phil has enforced for twenty-five years, not policies invented for a brochure.

Whether it's a full interior remodel of a Yountville cottage, a guest house for visiting family, or an outdoor kitchen built for entertaining, you get one accountable point of contact: Philip Howard, on your site, personally.

Building in Yountville: What We Know That Saves You Time

Yountville's compact footprint and design expectations shape every project. Here's what we bring to the table.

Permits & Jurisdiction

In-town projects run through the Town of Yountville's Planning & Building Department, which is small, personal, and detail-oriented — exactly the kind of counter where prepared, complete submittals move fast and sloppy ones stall. Surrounding vineyard parcels fall under Napa County PBES.

Design Review Awareness

Exterior changes in Yountville draw more scrutiny than in larger cities. We design additions and outdoor structures to complement the town's scale and character, which keeps approvals smooth and neighbors friendly.

Guest Houses & ADUs

With family and friends visiting wine country year-round, detached guest units are one of the most-requested projects here. State ADU law applies in Yountville just as it does everywhere in California — we handle feasibility through final inspection.

Yountville Homeowner Questions, Answered

Do you take on smaller remodels in Yountville, or only large projects?

Both. A precise bathroom or kitchen remodel in a Yountville cottage gets the same attention as an estate project — small square footage with high finish standards is honestly some of our favorite work.

How do you keep construction from disrupting the neighborhood?

Tight staging plans, daily site cleanup, respectful working hours, and crews who understand they're working in a hospitality town. Phil walks the site regularly and holds every sub to the same standard.

Can you build a guest house or ADU on my Yountville property?

In most cases yes. California ADU law applies statewide, and we'll verify Yountville's specific setback, height, and utility requirements at a free feasibility visit before you spend anything on plans.

Do you coordinate with architects and designers?

Constantly, and happily. Many Yountville projects arrive with an architect or interior designer already attached — we slot into that team, price the drawings honestly, and flag constructability issues early, before they become expensive.
